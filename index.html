WHY KALFRESH BIOENERGY

Kalfresh has farmed the Scenic Rim since 1992. Now we turn the region's crop residues and food waste into renewable gas, electricity and bio-fertiliser, and send the nutrients back to the paddock.

THE LOOP

From paddock to power and back to the paddock.

01 / Collect

Residues arrive

Crop residues and food waste from farms and processors.

02 / Digest and upgrade

Microbes break it down

Biogas is captured and cleaned to biomethane or burned for power.

03 / Return

Back to the soil

Digestate goes back to local paddocks as nutrient-rich bio-fertiliser.
