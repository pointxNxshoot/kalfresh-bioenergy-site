How it works

From paddock to power and back to the paddock.

Hover or tap a point on the map.

STAGE 01

Grow

Kalfresh grows carrots, sweet corn, beans, onions and more across the Scenic Rim. Every harvest leaves residues and rotational crops.

Crop residuesRotational cropsSince 1992
STAGE 02

Collect

Residues, rotational crops and food-processing waste from farms and manufacturers around Kalbar are collected and brought to the facility.

FarmsFood manufacturersCouncils
STAGE 03

Digest

Inside sealed tanks, microbes break the material down without oxygen and release biogas. The technology is delivered with WELTEC BIOPOWER.

Anaerobic digestionSealed tanksWELTEC BIOPOWER
STAGE 04

Upgrade and generate

Biogas is upgraded to renewable natural gas or used to generate electricity, with biogenic CO2 captured on the way.

Renewable natural gasElectricityBiogenic CO2
STAGE 05

Deliver to the precinct

Gas and power go to food manufacturers in the Scenic Rim Agricultural Industrial Precinct next door, and to the grid and transport fleets.

PrecinctGridTransport
STAGE 06

Return

The digestate left after digestion is a nutrient-rich fertiliser. It goes back onto local paddocks, closing the loop.

What we do

Kalfresh has farmed the Scenic Rim since 1992. Our bioenergy facility turns the region's crop residues and food waste into renewable gas and power, and returns the nutrients to the paddock as fertiliser.
